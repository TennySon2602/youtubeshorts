Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTAauto ("the Application", "we", "our") manages, processes, and protects user data when connecting to social media platforms including TikTok, YouTube, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), and X (Twitter).

1. Information We Collect

GTAauto is an automated content creation and publishing tool designed for gaming analysis and video shorts. We collect only the minimum data required to deliver publishing functionalities:

  • Authentication Credentials: OAuth access tokens and client credentials authorized directly by the account owner to upload and schedule videos.
  • Video Metadata: Titles, descriptions, hashtags, and video files provided for publication.

We do not collect, harvest, store, or sell personal user profiles, contacts, browsing histories, or private messages.

2. How We Use Information

Collected tokens are used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate API sessions with platform partners (TikTok Content Posting API, YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API, X API).
  • Publish user-approved video files and descriptive metadata to the user's connected social channels.

3. Data Storage & Security

All authentication tokens are stored locally or within encrypted environment secrets (such as GitHub Encrypted Secrets) accessible only by the application owner. We do not operate external third-party databases holding user login details.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is transmitted directly between the application client and official platform APIs over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

5. User Rights & Token Revocation

You have full control over your connected accounts at any time. You can immediately revoke GTAauto's access by visiting your account security settings on each respective platform:

  • TikTok: Settings and Privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Google / YouTube: Google Account Security → Third-party apps with account access.
  • Facebook & Instagram: Settings → Apps and Websites.
  • X: Settings and Support → Security and account access → Apps and sessions.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via our official GitHub repository: https://github.com/TennySon2602/youtubeshorts.
